SHIAI design system — bundled fonts
====================================

The font files in this directory are self-hosted (no CDN) to satisfy the
application's offline mandate and to avoid third-party requests (GDPR).
Each family is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(https://scripts.sil.org/OFL). Only the Latin subsets (full German coverage)
plus a small kanji subset of the decorative glyphs used in the UI are bundled.
